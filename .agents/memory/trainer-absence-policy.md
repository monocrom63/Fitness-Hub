---
name: Trainer absence policy
description: Manager ownership, privacy, availability, and notification-channel decisions for trainer absences.
---

Only managers report a trainer's sickness and record a replacement trainer's acceptance. A request to a candidate is an off-app coordination state, not an automated message or an in-app trainer account. An external trainer is manually recorded, not booked through a provider. Illness details belong in manager-only views and alerts; members receive only a neutral change or cancellation notice.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose manager-only operation and did not want trainers to have their own login. Members need actionable schedule changes, not health information.

**How to apply:** Keep request and confirmed-replacement states distinct. For availability, treat overlapping courses and a 30-minute changeover on either side as conflicts, and respect recorded absences. Deliver member updates in-app plus push where a subscription exists; do not present E-Mail or WhatsApp as delivered channels until those services are actually configured and verified.
